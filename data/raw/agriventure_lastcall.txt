## Technical Name : 

- Metarhizium anisopliae 1.15% WP (Wettable Powder)

## Mode of Action :

- Metarhizium anisopliae is an entomopathogenic fungus that infects insects through direct contact. The fungal spores attach to the insect body, germinate, penetrate the cuticle, and multiply inside the insect. The fungus feeds on the insect's body fluids, causing paralysis and death within a few days. After the insect dies, new fungal spores develop on the cadaver, helping spread control to other target pests naturally.

Application Recommendation :
