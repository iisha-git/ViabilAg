HEDO Verti is a biopesticide containing Verticillium Lecanii 1.15% WP, which is a beneficial fungus that acts as an eco-friendly pest control. This biopesticide targets a broad spectrum of pests, including aphids, mealybugs, thrips, whiteflies, and others, ensuring complete crop protection without harming beneficial insects. 

## Technical Content:

- Verticillium Lecanii 1.15% WP

## Key Features and Benefits:

- Broad-Spectrum: HEDO Verti is effective against a wide range of pests like Aphids, Thrips, Whiteflies, Jassids, and others.
- Organic Composition: Verticillium Lecanii 1.15% WP pesticide can be used for organic farming practices and IPM farming techniques. 
- Multiple Applications: HEDO pesticide can be applied through various methods, like seed treatment, foliar spray, and others. 
- Versatile Uses: The HEDO Verti can be used for gardening and farming practices, making this product highly versatile.

## Dose and Application Method: 

- For Seed Treatment: Use 10 gm in 50 ml of water and mix the seeds for 1 acre.
- For Field Application: Mix 1 kg with 100 kg for 1 acre.
- For Foliar Spray: Mix 20 gm in 1 liter of water.
- For Drip Irrigation: Use 1 kg per acre.

## Mode of Action: 

- Mode of Action: Contact 

## Suitable For: 

- The HEDO Verti is suitable for crops like Tomato, Potato, Brinjal, Banana, Papaya, Mango, Pomegranate, Bottle gourd, Bitter gourd, Ridge gourd, Cucumber, drumstick, and other plantation crops.

## Target Pests:

- Verticillium Lecanii 1.15% WP is effective against pests like aphids, thrips, whiteflies, jassids, mealybugs, tea mosquito bugs, leaf hopper, onion fly, and fungus gnats.
