Utkarsh Metarhoz - P (Metarhizium Anisopliae 1% W.P.) 

- Utkarsh Metarhoz-P is compliant with The Insecticide Act 1968 and is based on a selective strain of naturally-occurring entomopathogenic fungus Metarhizium Anisopliae 1%W.P., having CFU count of 1 x 10^8 per gram minimum, Strain: IPL/KC/44, Accession No ITCC 6895. Utkarsh Metarhoz-P is a biopesticide that works as a bio-insecticide prepared by the concept of Biotechnology. 
- When the spore of this fungus comes in contact with the cuticle (skin) of the target insect pests, it germinates and grows directly through the spiracle cuticle into the inner body of the host. The fungus proliferates throughout the insect’s body, draining the insect of nutrients and the infected insects eventually dies.
- Utkarsh Metarhoz – P effectively controls the fruit and shoot borer in Brinjal crop, leaf hoppers, Borers, cutworms, root grubs, termites, palm weevils etc. in various vegetable and fruit crops. 
- Application & Dosage: For controlling plant hoppers and bugs - Foliar Spray: Mix Metarhoz – P - 7-10 gm per Litre of water and spray this solution on plants. For controlling Grubs and Termites: Mix Metarhoz – P 1.5 Kg in 200 Litres of water (i.e 8 gm per Litre of water) and spray this solution on soil in 1 acre area. Do this application in the evening time after 5-6 pm. Maintain moisture for better results
- Utkarsh Metarhoz-P can also be used along with Utkarsh Beveroz and Utkarsh Verticoz for excellent results
- Target Crops: Tomato, Brinjal, Chilli, Capsicum, Okra, Pea, Cowpea, Coconut, Cotton, Maize, Wheat, Sugarcane, Rice, Potatoes, Sweet potatoes, Groundnuts, Soybean, Coffee, Cassava, Tea, Cocoa, Rubber, Oil palm, Some fruit trees like; Mango, Papaya, Citrus
