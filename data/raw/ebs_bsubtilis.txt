EBS Bacillus Subtilis is a biofungicide containing Bacillus Subtilis, which are beneficial bacteria that help slow down and stop fungal growth in crops. This fungicide can be used to prevent fungal and certain bacterial diseases like leaf blight, ensuring healthy crops. This fungicide is naturally derived, making it suitable for organic and IDM (Integrated Disease Management) farming practices.

## Technical Content:

- Bacillus Subtilis
- CFU - 2 x108 per ml 

## Key Features and Benefits:

- Two-in-One Action: Bacillus Subtilis fungicide also provides adequate protection against bacterial diseases, such as leaf blight.
- Organic Composition: EBS fungicide is made from natural components, making it compatible with organic farming practices.
- Versatile Application: Bacillus Subtilis fungicide can be applied using various methods, offering high versatility.
- Easy to Use: The liquid formulation of this product makes it easy to mix and apply through a sprayer for easy usage. 

## Dose and Application Method: 

- For Seed Treatment: Use 10 ml per kg of seed.
- For Foliar Application: Use 2.5 to 5 ml per liter of water.
- For Soil/Drip Application: Use 2.5 to 5 ml per liter of water.

## Suitable For:

## Target Diseases:
