## BIO POWER

BioPower is based on a beneficial entomophagous fungus Beauveria bassiana. It contains the spore and the mycelia fragments @ 1 X 10^8 cfu’s/gm of the product.

#### Benefits:

BioPower effectively controls most of the economically important pests such as Borers, Cut worms, Root grubs, Leaf hoppers, Whitefly, Aphids, Thrips, and Mealybug. BioPower helps to increase productivity by controlling the target pests efficiently. BioPower does not create resistance and resurgence of pests, rather it helps to significantly reduce pesticide residues in the environment.
