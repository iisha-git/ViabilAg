## About KATYAYANI PAECILOMYCES LILACINUS BIO FUNGICIDE POWDER

- Katyayani Paecilomyces Lilacinus is a Bio-nematicide in powder formulation containing high concentrations of natural live P. Lilacinus fungus.
- It is a naturally occurring fungus found in soils used as a nematicide and is applied to soil to control nematodes that attack plant roots.
- It is considered an eco-friendly alternative to chemicals.

## Katyayani Paecilomyces Lilacinus Technical Details

- Technical Name: Paecilomyces lilacinus powder formulation
- Mode of Action: Katyayani Paecilomyces lilacinus when applied to the soil or seeds it forms into contact with the nematode eggs, juveniles, or adult females and weakens and eventually kills the nematode.

## Key Features & Benefits

- Katyayani Paecilomyces Lilacinus controls nematodes, which are harmful to plant roots.
- This fungus is effective against a variety of nematodes, including root-knot nematodes, cyst nematodes, and lesion nematodes.
- Paecilomyces lilacinus controls the economically important nematodes like root knot nematodes, burrowing nematodes, cyst nematodes, lesion nematodes etc. among a wide range of crops.
- It is environment friendly and suitable for organic cultivation.

## Katyayani Paecilomyces Lilacinus Usage & Crops

Recommended crops: Maize, Sorghum, Soybean, Chickpea, Cow pea, Eggplant, Potato, Capsicum, Tomato, Cucumbers, Ornamental flowers, Vineyards Ornamentals in greenhouses and nurseries.

Target pests: Root knot nematodes (Meloidogyne spp.), Cyst Nematodes (Heterodera spp. and Globodera spp.), Root lesion nematodes (Pratylenchus spp.) Reniform Nematode (Rotylenchulus reniformis).

Method of application and Dosage

- Soil Application: Mix 10 kg of Paecilomyces lilacinus formulation with 100 kg FYM / well decomposed organic manure and apply around the rhizosphere uniformly for existing crops in the field for one hectare
- Drip System: Mix 10 kg of Paecilomyces lilacinus formulation with 1000 Liters of water and filter the contents well. After filtering it can be incorporated into the soil through the drip irrigation systems either before or after planting.

## Additional Information

- Paecilomyces Lilacinus is also effective on White Grubs and other soil borne pathogens.
