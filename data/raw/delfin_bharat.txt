##### Technical Name

Bacillus thuringiensis var. Kurstaki Serotype 3a, 3b, SA II WG

##### Product Detail

A biological insecticide to kill lepidopteran larvae with stomach poison activity.

##### Packing available

50 gm and 100 gm

##### Features and Benefits of Delfin WG:

• Delfin WG is a granular biological insecticide containing Bascilus thuringiensis kurstaki (Btk) strain, a stomach poison. • Btk Consisting of the solids active toxins, spores and subspecies for hard to kill insects such as Diamondback moth, Helicoverpa, Spodoptera and Lepidopteran caterpillars. • It is widely used in farming for suppression of insect population of lepidopteran larvae and pests in varying environment. • The potency of Delfin WG is 53,000 SU/mg and 32,000 IU/mg and is beneficial to soil bacteria. • Delfin WG is target specific hence no impact on non-target and beneficial insects. • Delfin WG is safer to environment, beneficial insects and hence has good fit in IPM & IRM.
