### Biofungicide with multiple modes of action to defy soil pathogens

- Protects crops against range of soilborne diseases – such as Fusarium, Pythium, Rhizoctonia and Phytophthora (check label for crop-disease combinations)
- Superior control – Trichoderma asperellum strain T34 outperforms many conventional / biological fungicides
- Fast protection - with high spore count, ASPERELLO ® T34 Biocontrol ® rapidly colonises the root zone forming a barrier against pathogens
- Kills pathogens – parasitises and actively kills pathogens while also triggering plant’s natural defense mechanisms
- Promotes healthy root and plant growth
- Superior performance in high temperatures
- Resistance risk small – due to multiple modes of action
- Convenient – shipped in small package ASPERELLO ® T34 Biocontrol® suspends easily in water.
