## Mildown Fungicide Uses — What Does It Control?

Mildown fungicide covers a broad range of fungal and bacterial diseases across all major crop types:

- Mildews: Downy Mildew, Powdery Mildew — Grapes, Cucurbits, Vegetables, Cereals
- Blights: Early Blight, Late Blight, Leaf Blight — Tomato, Potato, Cotton
- Rots: Root Rot, Stem Rot, Damping Off, Crown Rot — all vegetable and fruit crops
- Leaf Spots: Brown Leaf Spot, Ring Spot, Narrow Leaf Spot — Paddy, Tomato, Cotton
- Other Diseases: Anthracnose, Wilt, Rust, Scab, Die Back, Buck Eye Rot, Fruit Spots

## How To Use IPL Mildown Fungicide:

1. Measure the required dose of IPL Mildown as per the application method and crop stage above.
2. For foliar spray, mix 5–10 ml per litre in clean water, agitate well, and load into the sprayer.
3. Spray evenly on both upper and lower leaf surfaces — thorough coverage is essential for effective leaf colonisation.
4. For soil application, mix 1–2 litres per acre with 200 kg FYM or compost, broadcast evenly across the field, and follow with light irrigation.
5. For drip irrigation, introduce the required quantity through the drip system during the last 20–30 minutes of the irrigation cycle.
6. For seed treatment, mix 10 ml of Bacillus Subtilis 2.0% AS per kg of seeds using a mild jaggery solution, coat uniformly, and shade-dry for 30 minutes before sowing.
7. Do not mix IPL Mildown Fungicide with chemical fungicides or bactericides — these destroy the live bacteria and reduce product effectiveness.
8. Apply during early morning or late evening and rinse the sprayer thoroughly with clean water after use.

## Best Time to Apply:

Apply IPL Mildown in the early morning or late evening. Begin applications preventively before disease symptoms appear — especially during periods of high humidity and moderate temperature when fungal infections are most active. Preventive use gives far stronger and more consistent results than curative application.

## Frequency of Application & Re-Entry Period:

- Frequency of Application: Apply every 7–10 days during active disease pressure. Two to three applications per season are recommended for preventive management; increase frequency to every 7 days when disease symptoms are already visible.
- Re-Entry Period: IPL Mildown Fungicide is non-toxic. No strict re-entry restriction applies. Allow foliar spray to dry before re-entering the treated field.
- Tank-Mix Compatibility: Compatible with most biofertilizers, bio-pesticides, and organic inputs. Do not mix with chemical fungicides, bactericides, or strong chemical fertilizers — these destroy the live Bacillus subtilis bacteria. Always perform a jar test before mixing with unfamiliar products.

### Q1. What is IPL Mildown Fungicide used for?

IPL Mildown Fungicide controls Downy Mildew, Powdery Mildew, Early and Late Blight, Root Rot, Damping Off, Anthracnose, Wilt, Leaf Spots, and other seed, soil, and airborne fungal diseases. Bacillus Subtilis 2.0% AS also improves crop resistance to bacterial diseases.

### Q2. How does Bacillus Subtilis 2.0% AS work against fungal diseases?

Bacillus Subtilis 2.0% AS colonises root and leaf surfaces, competing with fungal pathogens for space and nutrients. It produces lipopeptide antibiotics — iturin, fengycin, and surfactin — that destroy fungal cell walls and spores. Simultaneously, it triggers Induced Systemic Resistance in the plant, making it naturally stronger against infection.

### Q3. Can IPL Mildown be used in organic farming?

Yes. IPL Mildown is classified under FRAC Group BM2, the Microbial Fungicide group, and is fully approved for certified organic farming. Bacillus Subtilis 2.0% AS is a naturally occurring organism that leaves no chemical residues in the soil or crop.

### Q4. Why should IPL Mildown Fungicide not be mixed with chemical fungicides?

Chemical fungicides, particularly copper-based, sulphur-based, and systemic fungicides, are bactericidal and will kill the live Bacillus subtilis bacteria in IPL Mildown fungicide, rendering the product completely ineffective. Always apply Mildown biological fungicide separately and allow at least 48 hours before or after any chemical fungicide application.

### Q5. How quickly does IPL Mildown show results on infected crops?

Mildown fungicide begins colonising leaf and root surfaces within 24–48 hours of application. Visible reduction in fungal disease spread is typically observed within 5–7 days of the first spray.
