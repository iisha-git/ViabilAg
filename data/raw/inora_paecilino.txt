### PAECILINO details:

PAECILINO is a biological nematicide containing Paecilomyces lilacinus that effectively suppresses plant-parasitic nematodes and promotes healthy root systems.

Note:

- Soil must be rich in Organic matter for Paecilomyces lilacinus to perform biocontrolling action.
- This should be used after 8 to 10 days of application of chemical fertilizers or pesticides.
