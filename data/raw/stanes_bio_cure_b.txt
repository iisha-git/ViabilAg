# BIO CURE-B

BioCure-B is based on an antagonistic beneficial rhizobacteria, Pseudomonas fluorescens. The product contains the bacterial cells at 1 x 10^8 cfu's/gm of the product.

### Features:

- Broad spectrum in action
- Offers long-lasting pest control
- Forms a good IDM and IRM molecule

### Benefits:

- BioCure-B effectively controls most of the economically important disease-causing pathogens such as Pyricularia oryzae, Alternaria sp., Mycosphaerella graminicola, Pythium sp., Rhizoctonia solani, Fusarium sp., Botrytis cinerea, Sclerotium rolfsii, and Sclerotinia homoeocarpa, which cause root rot, root wilt, seedling rot, and collar rot diseases in crops. BioCure-B helps to increase productivity by efficiently controlling the target disease-causing pathogens.

### Key Highlights


Mode of Action

BioCure-B controls pathogens through multiple modes of action.
