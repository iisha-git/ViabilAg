## BIO CATCH

BioCatch is based on a beneficial entomophagous fungus Verticillium lecanii. It contains the spore and the mycelia fragments @ 1 X 108 cfu’s/gm of the product.

#### Benefits:

BioCatch effectively controls most of the economically important sucking pests such as Whitefly, Jassids, Aphids, Thrips, Mealybug, etc. in a wide range of crops. BioCatch helps to increase the productivity by controlling the target pests efficiently. BioCatch does not create resistance and resurgence of pests but rather helps to significantly reduce pesticide residues in the environment.
