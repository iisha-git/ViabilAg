#### Product Description

- Farmroot HaNPV is a viral insecticide based Polyhedral Occluded Bodies (POB) of Nuclear Polyhedrosis Virus (NPV) of Helicoverpa armigera. After spraying of Farmroot Hanpv, the caterpillars get infected by the virus and eventually die. Within 3-4 days of spraying, the virus spreads to their next generation resulting in their control as well.
