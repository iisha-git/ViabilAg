## BIO MAGIC

BioMagic is based on a beneficial entomophagous fungus Metarhizium anisopliae. It contains the spore & mycelial fragments at 1 x 108 CFU’s/gm of the product.

#### Benefits:

BioMagic effectively controls most of the economically important pests such as Leaf hoppers, Grasshoppers, Root grubs, Corn root worms, Bugs, Beetles, Palm weevils, Borers, Cutworms, Termites, etc. BioMagic helps to increase productivity by controlling the target pests efficiently. It does not create resistance or resurgence of pests, helping to significantly reduce pesticide residues in the environment.


#### Mode of Action

- The spores and mycelial fragments contained in BioMagic take nutrients from the insect, proliferate, and colonize the entire insect. This process drains the insect of nutrients, and the infected insects eventually die.
