# Roadmap

Honest status: v0.2 reads labels, names the product type and shows cited evidence. It does **not** predict survival.

## Next (small)
- More real labels (target 100+ across 6-8 product types), re-measure accuracy.
- Hindi and other Indian languages in the UI and OCR.
- Photo-quality hints before OCR (blur and light check).
- Save a result as a shareable card.

## Later (needs data we do not have yet)
- **Survival estimate.** Needs lab measurements of live organisms after mixing (water pH, temperature, partners, time). Partner with a lab or agri-university; never ship a number without measured data behind it.
- Tank-mix partner checks with biological data.
- A product database (then revisit ADR 0003).

## Not planned
Telling farmers what to spray. bioOasis shows evidence; agronomists decide.
